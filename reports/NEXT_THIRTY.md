# Next thirty laboratories — v0.7

60 authored experiences; 180 assessment questions. This release adds the following original lessons, each with five guided chapters, SVG and WebGL views, interactive controls, numerical readouts, source links and explicit model limitations.

- **When moving bodies meet** (`collision`): Compare momentum transfer and kinetic-energy loss in a one-dimensional collision. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Balance around a pivot** (`lever`): Move the loads and compare clockwise and anticlockwise moments. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Read an invisible field** (`magnet`): Explore a magnetic dipole and use a movable compass probe to read field direction. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Keep a coil turning** (`motor`): See how a split-ring commutator keeps the motor torque turning the same way. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **The pattern that stands still** (`standing`): Find nodes and antinodes as two opposite travelling waves form a standing wave. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Measure with a returning sound** (`echo`): Send a sound pulse to a wall and use its round-trip delay to infer distance. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Heat finds a balance** (`conduction`): Connect two insulated blocks and follow thermal energy toward equilibrium. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Water climbs a narrow tube** (`capillary`): Compare a wetting rise with a non-wetting depression in a capillary. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **A clock inside the nucleus** (`decay`): Follow an ensemble through repeated half-lives and distinguish expected counts from single events. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **An orbit that changes speed** (`orbit`): Follow an elliptical orbit and compare the near-Sun and far-Sun parts of the journey. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Find the turning point of a titration** (`titration`): Add a strong base to a strong acid and follow stoichiometry, pH and the equivalence point. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Follow the more volatile component** (`distillation`): Watch a binary mixture boil, condense and form an enriched distillate. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Build the space inside a solid** (`crystal`): Assemble simple, body-centred and face-centred cubic cells and compare packing. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Split water with charge** (`electrolysis`): Compare charge passed with the hydrogen and oxygen produced in ideal water electrolysis. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **A chemical source of voltage** (`galvanic`): Follow electrons through a Daniell cell and see how concentration changes its voltage. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **A small target, a large deflection** (`rutherford`): Send an alpha particle past a gold nucleus and compare impact parameter with scattering angle. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **A gas dissolves under pressure** (`solubility`): Change gas pressure and follow a dissolved concentration toward its Henry-law limit. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Separate with a spinning frame** (`centrifuge`): Track ideal particles moving outward in a centrifuge and compare size, density and speed. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **One beat, two circulations** (`heart`): Follow blood through four heart chambers, the lungs and the body circuit. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Filter, reclaim, excrete** (`nephron`): Follow filtrate through a schematic nephron and balance water and a freely filtered solute. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **The short route to a response** (`reflex`): Follow a simplified sensory-spinal-motor pathway and compare signal travel delays. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Copy a strand, keep the code** (`replication`): Move a replication fork and check complementary bases in a semiconservative copy. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Two divisions, four haploid sets** (`meiosis`): Follow homolog separation and sister separation to four haploid sets. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **From roots to the air** (`transpiration`): Connect stomatal opening and humidity with an illustrative leaf-water loss rate. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Energy through an ecosystem** (`foodweb`): Follow one food-chain pathway and compare transferred with untransferred energy. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Growth meets a limit** (`population`): Compare early growth with the slowing effect of a fixed carrying capacity. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **The same water, many journeys** (`watercycle`): Transfer water among ocean, air, land and ice while conserving the model’s total. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Tilt changes the sunlight** (`seasons`): Change tilt, latitude and season to compare day length and noon Sun altitude. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Read the Moon’s changing face** (`moon`): Connect Sun–Earth–Moon geometry with the illuminated fraction seen from Earth. [Scientific reference](#company).
- **Two arrivals from one quake** (`seismic`): Compare P and S arrivals and infer source distance from their time separation. [Scientific reference](#company).

## Scope and review

Animations are slowed or compressed explanatory models; physical time and assumptions appear in the scene. Not every scene is a predictive biological solver or anatomically validated asset. All authored lessons remain pending independent scientific expert review. Editorial grade recommendations are not official board mappings. Existing 55 partial curriculum evidence links remain separate. New source links reference OpenStax, NASA and USGS; the USGS water-cycle page required browser verification during source retrieval.

The 408 concepts are inventory records, not 408 completed laboratories. Subsequent authoring will be tracked in batches of 25 without silently marking related experiences as full concept completion.
