# Ecology, health and mechanics visual upgrade — batch 03

SA-0051–SA-0075: 25 separately checked concept routes. Each retains a 100-second presentation, both viewers, guided captions, an observation table and an exam-style practical report. Static explanatory stages remain static where motion would misrepresent the concept.

## Changes

Ecology viewers gain common-scale energy budgets, transfer routes with conserved reservoir amounts, a logistic curve with a current-state marker, and input/removal apparatus. Energy bar heights are linear without minimum-height exaggeration. Zero source/removal rates suppress the corresponding added traffic. Population growth units are individuals/day. Food, nutrient and public-health diagrams gain structural context; these remain teaching illustrations rather than dietary, diagnostic or risk predictors. Adolescence uses a clothed human outline with explicit limits on interpreting developmental variation. Pathogen stages now select their intended representative organisms.

Mechanics gains track graduations, contact surfaces, an external force actuator with an equal-and-opposite partner, recent trajectories, pendulum-frame feet, pivot/load details and a clearer orbital camera. Free-fall stroboscopic positions use equal physical-time intervals from the same analytic model. Projectile and collision concept clocks follow ordered trials so the captions agree with approach, collision and contact. The final torque trial is balanced; friction distinguishes static, limiting, sliding and reversed conditions. The final acceleration/free-fall chapters select drag and mass comparisons respectively.

Spring views display signed instantaneous power transferred from spring to mass. The working guide substitutes captured time, displacement, velocity, force, power and energies with units; older trials lacking a model clock omit instantaneous calculations. A finite-difference test checks power against the rate of kinetic-energy change. Repeated render-frame descriptions are reused across the notebook, toolbar and viewer, with cache invalidation for time and parameter changes.
