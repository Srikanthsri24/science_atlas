# Fluids and electromagnetism visual upgrade — batch 04

SA-0076–SA-0100: 25 separately checked concept routes, each with both viewers, a 100-second presentation, guided captions, a practical observation table and an exam-style report. Static reference stages remain static where continuous material motion would misrepresent the science.

## Changes

Air-resistance lessons follow ordered descents and separate area/mass comparison trials. Equal-time position markers, drag/weight ratios and airborne acceleration make the approach to terminal speed visible. The terminal-velocity caption now says that drag approaches weight; a finite tower does not guarantee exact terminal speed. The satellite lesson retains its explicitly stated solar-mass/AU two-body illustration; it does not claim Earth-satellite altitude calculations. Escape-velocity views gain signed radial velocity and inward attraction guides; unrelated horizontal traffic was removed.

Buoyancy apparatus gains depth-dependent pressure guides, top/bottom gauge-pressure values and a straight support frame. Immersion stages now distinguish zero, partial and full displacement, and the apparent-weight lesson ends with a downward-restraint case. A percentage is labelled “Submerged fraction,” while displaced volume retains litres. Capillary views gain a glass tube, spherical-cap meniscus guides and an enlarged contact-angle inset measured through the liquid. Wetting, 90° and non-wetting stages select positive, zero and negative equilibrium heights. The transient remains explicitly illustrative, with different horizontal and vertical scales.

The Bernoulli bench gains mounting supports and pipe flanges while preserving its continuity-based tracers and pressure heads. The viscosity lesson gains a co-rotating radial inset. Its shared reference clock fixes the previous normalization that made different viscosities appear to settle identically. Pressure-transmission highlights replace apparent hydraulic circulation. Zero input force suppresses force arrows, and zero gauge depth suppresses the corresponding probe-pressure arrows.

Motor coil geometry, side currents and magnetic-force directions now agree with the model turning tendency. The geometric reference normal is drawn and its angle to B is labelled. Bearings, windings, brushes, split rings and supply leads clarify the apparatus. Current and force guides disappear at zero current. The DC battery polarity and open-switch geometry are corrected; the switch stays visibly open throughout an open trial. Generator views gain supports, a signed-emf dial and flux/current values; their 2D coil outline matches the rectangular 3D example. Transformer views gain source/load connections, winding leads and core laminations; symbolic flux direction reverses with the AC half-cycle and vanishes at zero flux.

## Practical working

Captured-state guides substitute motor drive/damping torque, angular acceleration and drive power; radial migration position/speed; and drag force, acceleration and drag/weight ratio. Hydraulic work includes an explicitly hypothetical 10 mm input stroke rather than claiming observed piston travel. Model-clock omissions in older records remain explicit. Geometry additions do not introduce a hidden new numerical solver.
