CL-0083 · Use chromatography to compare ink samples

Aim,apparatus and method

Four-lane paper,covered chamber,solvent reservoir,pencil origin and ruler. Generic original reference inks;no commercial-brand identification.

  1. Aim: compare an unknown reference ink with known lanes under controlled paper-chromatography conditions.
  2. Place A,B,C and U on one pencil origin2cm above the paper base;the chosen solvent pool reaches0.8cm. Keep the ink origin above the pool.
  3. Use one solvent system for the whole sheet. Follow the solvent front and record its distance from the same origin used for every band.
  4. Develop the front by at least4cm and check separation. This virtual comparison withholds individual readings when the nominal Gaussian resolution is below1.5. Overlap is inconclusive evidence,not proof of a single dye.
  5. Read the lower and upper resolved band centres with the selected ruler increment. These are model band-centre readings,not calibrated real ink measurements.
  6. Calculate Rf=band travel/front travel for each reportable band. Both distances start at the pencil origin;do not divide by total paper height or time.
  7. Compare each reference with U on the same sheet. Two half-step band-distance allowances sum to one ruler increment. A consistent pattern is evidence of consistency,not unique identification.
  8. Prepare a fresh run with the second chosen solvent system and the same unknown vial. Keep early/unresolved trials as such;do not treat them as mismatches.
  9. Write aim,apparatus,method,observations,worked Rf calculations,result,conclusion and limitations. State which references remain consistent across one or both conditions and whether any eligible comparison excluded them.

Prediction and plan
