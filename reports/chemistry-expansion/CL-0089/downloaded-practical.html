Cooling curve and freezing plateau

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited

Aim

To obtain sample and finite-cold-store temperature curves,observe the freezing plateau,and reconcile phase masses and total energy.

Student hypothesis and plan

Aim: compare both temperatures at 60 s intervals, then change cold-store capacity. Predict freezing only when the remaining cold reserve is positive. <safe writing>

Apparatus and reference preparation

A liquid-water sample at20°C,a vessel with a chosen effective heat capacity,a generic finite cold store and a thermal contact. The pair is insulated from the room. The store is not a named material or subzero pure-water bath.

Procedure and observation instructions

  1. Aim: obtain sample and cold-store cooling curves,identify a freezing plateau,and test whether the available cold-store capacity permits no freezing,partial freezing or complete freezing.
  2. Record water mass,vessel heat capacity,cold-store heat capacity and starting temperature,and thermal contact. The sample starts as liquid water at20°C. The cold store is a generic chosen reference,not liquid water kept below0°C.
  3. Record both temperatures at equal physical-time intervals. The presentation timeline differs from the physical clock. Changes to preparation start a fresh experiment.
  4. Inspect the predicted0°C onset only if a finite event occurs within the run. At a capacity threshold,the approach can be asymptotic rather than a finite freezing event.
  5. During the plateau,record liquid and ice masses separately. Observe the cold store warming as it receives sensible and latent heat. A plateau does not prove that all sample water has frozen.
  6. If complete freezing occurs,record the completion event and subsequent ice cooling. If only a partial limit is possible,retain the remaining liquid rather than declaring completion.
  7. Calculate heat capacities,cold reserve,event times,mass balance and energy exchange. Use a single enthalpy reference:ice at0°C=0,liquid at0°C=mL. Signed stored enthalpy is a reference value,not negative heat capacity.
  8. Compare total current energy with total initial energy,and sample heat release with cold-store heat gain. The pair is insulated in this reference;no heat disappears into an untracked external bath.
  9. Repeat at common physical times while changing one condition. Include a disconnected-contact case and capacity thresholds. Write the result with units and restrict the conclusion to the stated lumped model.
