CL-0076 · Physical and chemical changes: particle evidence

Aim,apparatus and procedure

Virtual isothermal gas chamber,piston,pressure/volume readers,molecular accounting viewer and conserved-element counters.

  1. Aim: distinguish a physical operation from chemical conversion using molecular identity and conserved atoms.
  2. Prepare the same initial amounts for both comparisons. Record initial species, pressure, volume and total mass.
  3. Move the physical piston. Record at least three volume ratios and check that species amounts remain unchanged.
  4. Reset to the reference volume for chemical comparison. Choose several prescribed extent fractions; record reactants, products and each element account.
  5. Apply the balanced equation and worked substitutions. Compare total mass with the initial value. A pressure change alone does not establish chemical conversion.
  6. Conclude from species evidence. State that the molecular transition is an illustrative accounting storyboard, not a measured reaction path, ignition experiment or kinetic model.

Prediction and plan

Compare unchanged species under compression with new product species at fixed temperature. Check all element accounts.

Student conclusion and limitations

Species identity changes during prescribed conversion; C, H, O and total mass remain conserved. Pressure alone is insufficient evidence.

At600K,all specified species are treated as ideal gases. Chemical extent is prescribed,not predicted from time. Thermostat heat,ignition,flame,kinetics,equilibrium,dissociation and elementary mechanism are outside this model. Molecular accounting arrows/glyphs are not microscopic trajectories. Chemical identity is the criterion; neither reversibility nor pressure alone is decisive. Internal educational model; editorial class placement.
