Investigate temperature and gas solubility

CL-0069 · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · Virtual practical

Aim

Compare oxygen partition between water and a sealed finite headspace during a slow prescribed temperature change, with a matched constant-temperature reference.

Principle and apparatus

Matched rigid sealed chambers, water, oxygen and a hypothetical nonabsorbing dry carrier, thermostatted jackets and diagnostic thermometers/pressure gauges. Approximate oxygen Henry reference kH298=0.0013mol/(kg·bar), temperature constant1500K. Henry equilibrium and ideal gases conserve total oxygen while allowing oxygen partial pressure to change. Total dry pressure includes carrier and oxygen, not water vapour. Dissolved oxygen is not a visible bubble layer.

Procedure

Prepare matched water/headspace volumes, initial temperature and oxygen partial pressure. Record both initial equilibria. Slowly warm the programmed chamber while holding the reference at its initial temperature. Pause at matched programme times; record temperature, dissolved oxygen, gas oxygen and separate oxygen/total dry pressure readings. Repeat cooling, equal-temperature and smaller/larger headspace controls. Apply worked formulas with units. Check total oxygen amount and mass; explain signed release into gas or uptake into water. State the approximate equilibrium assumptions.
