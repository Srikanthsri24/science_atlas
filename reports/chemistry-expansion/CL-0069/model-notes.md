# CL-0069 — temperature and gas solubility (design/core stage)

Original editorial Class5 investigation; quantitative Henry/ideal-gas analysis is a teacher extension. Compare two sealed matched water/headspace chambers, one held at its initial temperature and one slowly following a prescribed smooth temperature programme. Water50–150mL; fixed gas headspace50–150mL; initial10–25°C; programme target5–30°C; chosen initial dry oxygen partial pressure0.1–0.3bar. Both begin at their own Henry equilibrium. Initial oxygen inventory N= kH(T0)mw p0 + p0 Vg/(RT0). At any prescribed uniform T, dissolved oxygen nd=kH(T)mw p, gas oxygen ng=pVg/(RT), and p=N/[kH(T)mw+Vg/(RT)]. Thus changing oxygen partition does not silently hold the finite sealed headspace partial pressure fixed. Inert carrier pressure follows ncarrierRT/Vg; displayed total dry pressure is carrier plus oxygen. Neither total pressure nor oxygen pressure is a claim of ordinary air saturation at fixed atmospheric pressure. Dissolved oxygen is molecular solute, not drawn liquid bubbles or a separated blue oxygen layer.
