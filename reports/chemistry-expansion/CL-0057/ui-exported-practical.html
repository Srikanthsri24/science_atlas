Compare water samples using a turbidity model

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0057 · Class5 editorial investigation

Aim

Compare direct and right-angle light with matched sample preparation and elapsed time. Distinguish settling particles, dissolved colour and relative optical clarity.

Apparatus and model

100mL reference cells, source, direct detector, perpendicular side detector and retained sediment bed. The chamber is rescaled independently on display; use numerical path values rather than measuring screen lengths. Chosen exponential settling and single-scatter optical models are not calibrated natural-water or instrument predictions.

Prediction

At matched path and elapsed time, faster settling should reduce suspended particles and increase direct transmission. Retained bed mass should account for the change. <check> & "units"

Procedure

  1. Record the reference blank and source power. Keep cell path and detector geometry matched.
  2. Prepare100mL final sample from100/dmL aliquot and the remaining blank water. Dilution also reduces colour absorption.
  3. Record initial concentration, chosen settling rate, colour, dilution, path and physical time.
  4. Run and pause the investigation. Record suspended and retained bed masses, direct light, right-angle signal and blank-relative transmission.
  5. Repeat at matched times. Compare slow/faster settling and a dissolved-colour sample. Avoid calling lower transmission a unique measure of particles.
  6. Substitute values with units. State whether the comparison was controlled and explain the limits.
