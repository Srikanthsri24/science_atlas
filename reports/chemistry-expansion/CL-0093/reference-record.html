Paired litmus tests · practical record

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited

Aim

Compare both fresh litmus papers with chosen acid,base and water controls,and record an evidence-limited inference.

Student hypothesis and plan

Test both fresh papers.

Apparatus

Prepared reference sample,clean applicators,separate fresh red and blue papers,white test tile and observation notebook.

Procedure

  1. Aim: use both fresh red and blue litmus papers to compare chosen acid,base and water references,and state the limits of the inference.
  2. Place separate red and blue papers on a clean white tile. Record their starting colours. Use a clean applicator for each reference and never taste a test sample.
  3. Record the chosen prepared reference,stock concentration and dilution factor where applicable,sample volume and the planned aliquot volume for each paper. Water is a separate zero-solute control;its stock settings are unused.
  4. Apply the reference aliquot to red paper. Inspect and record the indicator area only. A clear bulk solution does not become red or blue just because the paper changes colour.
  5. Apply a separate clean aliquot to fresh blue paper. Record its observation too. A partial contact stage is not a completed indicator reading.
  6. Compare the completed pair with acid,base and water controls. Blue-to-red supports an acid endpoint;red-to-blue supports a base endpoint. Unresolved transition colours do not provide a confident classification.
  7. Unchanged starting colours in the known water control do not establish that an unknown has exactly pH7. Litmus is not an exact pH meter.
  8. Optional advanced calculations: convert mM to mol/L,apply the chosen dilution,and include water autoionization in the ideal25°Cstrong-acid/base charge balance. Calculate pH from the model concentrations,not from a paper RGB colour.
  9. Account for sample volume and reference-solute moles in the remaining reservoir and two delivered aliquots. Individual H⁺ and OH⁻ inventories are not conserved chemical species because water equilibrates.
  10. Repeat with one changed preparation and fresh papers. Write the inference,contrasting evidence and limitations in your conclusion. Staged contact timing is not measured indicator kinetics or absorption dynamics.
