Compare evaporation in moving and still air

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0051 · Class4 editorial investigation

Aim

Compare equal starting water and exposed areas under prescribed still-air and moving-air films. Distinguish physical time,net flux,remaining liquid and finite drying.

Apparatus and model

Two equal-area circular receivers at maintained25°C,matched ambient humidity,and an illustrative fan. The still reference film is10mm;the moving reference film is chosen between2 and10mm. These are prescribed model conditions,not inferred fan-speed measurements. D=0.000025m²/s,saturation concentration0.023kg/m³,density0.997g/mL and latent heat2442000J/kg are representative rounded teaching constants. The linear diffusion-film approximation is J=(D/δ)(csat−c∞). Water vapour is invisible;film colours and tracers are explanatory.

Prediction

With equal initial water and area,the thinner prescribed moving-air film should give greater net water loss while both samples remain wet. At equal25°C and100%RH,I expect zero net loss.

Procedure

  1. Choose equal initial water volumes and equal exposed areas. Keep both air and water surfaces at the same25°C.
  2. Record ambient RH and the prescribed moving-air film thickness;the still reference remains10mm. Predict net loss before running.
  3. Watch,pause and record both samples at matched physical hours. The120s guide compresses24 physical hours into90 moving seconds plus setup/conclusion holds. Manual120-second playback spans72 physical hours. Do not substitute presentation seconds into a formula using hours.
  4. Record remaining and evaporated mass,depth,current rate and latent replenishment. Check remaining plus escaped water equals initial water.
  5. Compare equal films,thinner moving films and a100%RH case. Matched-temperature saturation gives zero net flux;individual molecules can still exchange.
  6. Continue until a sample dries. Its actual rate and latent demand then become zero,even if its wet-reference rate is positive.
  7. Use the formula substitutions below and support a conclusion with matched trials.

Result and conclusion

Matched trials showed faster wet-sample loss for a thinner reference film,equal evolution for equal films,and zero net loss at matched-temperature saturation. Dry samples stopped losing mass. Remaining plus escaped water conserved the starting inventory. Prescribed films do not calibrate fan speed or predict weather.

Precautions and limitations

This is an original prescribed-film teaching comparison,not a weather or fan-speed forecast. Constant temperature,density,area and ambient humidity are assumed. The film does not dynamically develop. Natural convection,Stefan flow,turbulence,humidity buildup,contact-line recession,cooling and apparatus heat capacity are omitted. Ideal latent replenishment is not total electrical input. Still air does not mean still molecules. Air does not literally hold water vapour. Evaporation is not boiling;vapour is invisible.
