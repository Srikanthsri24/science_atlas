Read a meniscus at different eye heights

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0046 · Class 4 editorial investigation

Aim

Keep a reference water volume fixed, compare above/level/below eye positions, and separate geometric viewing error from rounding.

Apparatus and model

Original virtual 100 mL cylinder, 1 mL divisions, known reference water, central meniscus target, movable observer and geometric sight line. A front scale plane lies 1.3 cm ahead of the target; a back-plane extension is a reference construction. Straight rays omit refraction. The prescribed curved water surface preserves contained volume.

Prediction

With water fixed, an eye above the central target should give a higher front-scale geometric indication. Alignment should remove that geometric error; greater horizontal distance should reduce it.

Procedure

  1. Select a known fixed water volume and horizontal observer distance. Identify the lowest central meniscus target.
  2. Set an eye position above the target. Read the magnified front-scale marker and enter an estimate before revealing the computed reading.
  3. Reveal the geometric indication, then record the paired front/back references, rounded front reading and errors.
  4. Repeat at eye level and below while holding water volume and distance fixed.
  5. Repeat near and far at the same eye offset. Compare error against inverse horizontal distance.
  6. Watch the 80-second sweep or 120-second guide. Pause before recording a moving-observer state. The water stays still.
  7. Write a supported conclusion. Distinguish a geometric scale shift from an actual volume change, and rounding resolution from total uncertainty.
