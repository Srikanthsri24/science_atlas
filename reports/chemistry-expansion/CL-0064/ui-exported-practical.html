Rust formation: water and oxygen conditions

CL-0064 · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · Virtual practical

Aim

Compare equal iron samples under four water/oxygen reference conditions. Follow iron consumption and corrosion products, and distinguish coupon mass from whole-cell mass.

Principle and apparatus

Chosen equivalent reaction:4Fe+3O₂+2H₂O→4FeOOH. Real rust is a mixture. Four rigid sealed reference cells, equal iron preparations, available-water pools with schematic wicks, normal/reduced-oxygen O₂/N₂ reference gas, timer and calculated dry-gas pressure.

Procedure

Record iron mass, cell volume, available water, oxygen fractions, chosen rate and attached fraction. Record all four initial conditions. Run, pause and record each condition on matched days. Inspect the shrinking metal core, attached equivalent-volume layer and separately retained sediment. Repeat a small residual-oxygen reference, water-limited preparation and different product partitions. Apply formulas with units; compare coupon, all iron-containing phases and whole-cell mass. Write result, conclusion and limits; ideal excluded controls are not proof that classroom drying or deoxygenation is perfect.
