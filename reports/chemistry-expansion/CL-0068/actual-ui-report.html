Compare solution concentration by colour intensity

CL-0068 · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · Virtual practical

Aim

Compare an unchanged reference with a water-diluted hypothetical coloured solution. Relate conserved solute amount, solution volume, concentration and monochromatic absorbance. Test the effect of optical path length.

Principle and apparatus

Two rectangular cells, a finite water syringe, ideal monochromatic source and calculated power detector. Solute amount is fixed in each cell. A=εcl and T=10⁻ᴬ for a hypothetical stable absorber with chosen ε=20000L/(mol·cm). This is not a calibrated named-dye spectrum or human colour prediction. Liquid tint and beam arrows are explanatory overlays.

Procedure

Prepare equal initial volumes and concentrations; begin with equal1cm optical paths. Record both initial references. Transfer pure water to one cell over80physicalseconds, pause and record matched times. Note cell volume, remaining syringe water, concentration, absorbance and transmitted/absorbed power. Check conserved solute and combined liquid volume. Repeat zero-addition and clear-solvent controls. Change the diluted-cell path and explain why equal apparent attenuation need not mean equal concentration. Substitute values with units, then write result, conclusion and limitations.

Prediction
