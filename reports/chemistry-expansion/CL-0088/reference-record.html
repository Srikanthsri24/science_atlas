Water heating curve and boiling plateau

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited

Aim,hypothesis and plan

SYNTHETIC calculation inspection,not actual GUI observations.

Apparatus and reference preparation

Virtual well-mixed open water vessel,heater with a chosen coupled fraction,chosen vessel heat capacity and linear room heat-loss coefficient. Initial water and ambient temperature20°C. Rounded reference boiling pair100°C/101420Pa. This model is not an instruction for physical boiling apparatus.

Procedure and observation instructions

  1. Aim: obtain a heating curve for liquid water,identify whether boiling is reached,and explain where the heater energy goes. Predict how water amount or heat loss changes the curve.
  2. Record the initial water mass,heater source power,coupled fraction,heat-loss coefficient and vessel heat capacity. Water and room both begin at20°C. Parameter changes start a fresh reference experiment.
  3. Observe temperature at equal physical-time intervals during warming. Plot temperature vertically and physical time horizontally;the presentation timeline is not the physical clock.
  4. Inspect the predicted boiling onset if it lies within the run. The rounded reference is100°C at101420Pa. If input cannot exceed heat loss at that temperature,record that no finite boiling event occurs.
  5. During boiling,record remaining liquid and escaped vapour separately at two or more times. A flat temperature trace does not mean that the heater stopped transferring energy.
  6. Calculate electrical input,coupled heat,uncoupled energy,room heat loss,water warming,vessel warming and latent energy. Check the source budget and sample budget separately.
  7. For the open system,split the sensible warming into remaining-water inventory and sensible energy carried by escaped water. Do not count only the remaining liquid when checking the whole-run energy balance.
  8. At dryout the reference investigation stops. Record that liquid temperature is no longer available;the last valid model temperature is not a measurement of nonexistent liquid. Cooling of empty glass is outside this model.
  9. Repeat at a common physical time while changing one preparation parameter. Write the result with temperature,time,mass and energy units,then explain the curve and limitations without claiming apparatus calibration.

Precautions and model limitations

Constant water Cp4180J/(kg·K) is a teaching approximation;latent2256400J/kg and boiling pressure101420Pa are rounded reference values at100°C. Coupling,heat-loss coefficient and vessel capacity are chosen references,not measured calibration. Liquid-volume geometry uses piecewise-linear saturation-density anchors at20,40,60,80,100°C,not a fixed-pressure density equation or calibrated meniscus. No below-boiling evaporation,thermal gradients,probe lag,pressure variation or nucleation dynamics. Water and vessel share a single temperature. After dryout the experiment ends;subsequent empty-vessel cooling is not evolved. No current liquid temperature exists when empty. Heat loss at the last temperature is not physically zero;the accounted post-stop transfer is zero because the model clock has stopped. All initial water warming includes sensible energy later carried out by vapour. Changing preparation starts a fresh experiment. Class path is editorial;internal engineering checks are not independent expert or official curriculum certification.
