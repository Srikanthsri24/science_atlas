Two salt solutions and a precipitate

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0102

Aim and prediction

Compare matched stocks, one-ion controls and excess reagent. Literal <AgCl> & recorded values.

Scientific principle

Net ionic equilibrium: Ag⁺(aq)+Cl⁻(aq) ⇌ AgCl(s). Sodium and nitrate remain dissolved spectator material in this ideal model.

Apparatus and procedure

  1. Write an aim and prediction. Identify the two separate stock solutions and the pure-water receiver. This record describes a supervised virtual investigation.
  2. Record stock volumes,formal concentrations,receiver water and the delivered fraction from each burette. Zero concentration means a water-control stock that still transfers liquid.
  3. Deliver silver-nitrate stock first. Note that one reactive ion alone does not produce AgCl solid;the nitrate remains in the receiver.
  4. Add sodium-chloride stock and compare both delivered amounts. Record precipitate,remaining free ions and spectator sodium/nitrate.
  5. Calculate the pre-precipitation ion product and compare it with the stated Ksp. Use the coupled phase-volume equilibrium model;do not assume every delivered ion becomes solid.
  6. Repeat with a reagent in excess,dilute stocks and a zero-concentration control. Apply the formulas using your recorded values and write a result,conclusion and limitations.
