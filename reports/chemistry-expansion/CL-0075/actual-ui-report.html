CL-0075 · Model diffusion in gases and liquids

Aim, apparatus and procedure

Apparatus: two identical sealed rectangular channels, central ideal partitions, virtual clocks and concentration/half-mass readers.

  1. Aim: compare diffusion of generic dilute passive tracers in sealed gas and liquid channels.
  2. Keep channel length, cross-sectional area and tracer mass identical. Record the chosen diffusion coefficients; they are teaching inputs, not measured material properties.
  3. Begin with the partition closed. Record the initial left and right masses and zero cross-partition transfer.
  4. Remove the ideal central partition. Under equal clocks, pause at several times and record both physical clocks, half-channel masses and net flux. Change only one independent variable per comparison.
  5. Reset for the matched-progress trial. Compare at the same Dt/L² and record the different physical times. Never label these observations as equal-time trials.
  6. Use the worked substitutions to check volume, concentration, transfer rate and total tracer conservation. Copy units into every table heading.
  7. Conclusion: compare transfer at equal physical times, then explain why matching Dt/L² produces matching profiles. State that convection, reactions and chemical-specific properties were excluded.

Student prediction and plan

Compare equal preparations at equal clocks, then reset for matched Dt/L².
