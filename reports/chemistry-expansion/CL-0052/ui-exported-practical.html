Follow the path of water through a simple filter bed

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0052 · Class 4 editorial investigation

Aim

Track a labelled water parcel through a prewetted three-layer reference bed. Identify the water already in the pores, delayed labelled arrival and water retained after flow stops.

Apparatus and model

Two finite reference feed sources, an ideal controlled inlet-pressure connection, a saturated 6 cm column with 2 cm each of gravel, sand and carbon-granule reference material, and a collecting receiver. Inlet/outlet isolation valves close at feed exhaustion to retain pore water. Ideal connections have zero modelled holdup;there is no extra unaccounted pond. Chosen conductivities0.002/0.0005/0.001 m/s and connected porosities0.4/0.35/0.5 are teaching parameters, not measured material specifications. Colour labels water paths;it is not a contaminant or purity measurement. The receiver is represented as uniformly mixed.

Prediction

I expect initially resident pore water to leave before the labelled front. A sufficient clear chase should move all labelled water into the receiver;with too little chase,some label should remain when the valves close.

Procedure

  1. Record area, inlet pressure head, labelled feed and clear chase. Calculate the water already filling the connected pores.
  2. Start the labelled feed, then the clear chase. Keep the chosen pressure head controlled while feed remains.
  3. Pause at physical seconds before and after the labelled front reaches the receiver. Early outlet water can come from the prewetted bed.
  4. Record current flow, interval-average flow, receiver volumes and labelled water in each layer/source. Presentation seconds are not physical seconds:the 120 s guide compresses60 physical seconds into90 moving seconds plus holds;manual 120s spans600 physical seconds.
  5. Repeat with zero chase. Labelled water may remain trapped when both valves close;do not assume all injected label reaches the receiver.
  6. Apply the substitutions and check both whole-water and labelled-parcel budgets. Include the initial pore water.
  7. Compare area and inlet-head changes. Explain delayed arrival and retained water using your observations.

The early trial collected32mL of resident water before any label emerged. The complete reference run collected300mL,including100mL labelled,while70mL remained in the bed. Precautions and limitations

Prewetted, saturated, connected pores andquasi-steady Darcy flow are assumed. Ideal piston transport omits dispersion, diffusion, bypass, clogging, air entrapment anddry-bed wetting. No particle capture, adsorption orcontaminant removal is simulated. Clear-looking water is not evidence of drinking safety. Grain meshes andcolour bands are explanatory, not a literal packing or chemical analysis. Shutdown pressure transients andconnection dead volumes are omitted. No physical calibration, official-board mapping orindependent expert certification is claimed.
