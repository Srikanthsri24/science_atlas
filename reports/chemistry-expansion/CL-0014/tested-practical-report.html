Recover visible solids by handpicking

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs · CL-0014 · Class 2

Aim

Separate visible grains and stones and distinguish recovery from purity.

Apparatus and principle

A dry mixture of 18 model grains (0.4 g each) and 6 model stones (1.2 g each), three sorting trays and a picking tool. Differences in visible size, shape and colour permit manual selection; material identity and mass are conserved.

Prediction

Visible stones can be picked out without changing the identity or total mass of any piece.

Procedure

  1. Inspect the mixture and record its initial masses.
  2. Select a visible object and move it to the appropriate tray.
  3. Record after one correctly recovered grain; compare purity and recovery.
  4. Deliberately place a stone in the grain tray, record contamination, then correct it.
  5. Finish separating all objects. Record mass balance, recovery, purity and a limitation.

Result and conclusion

The completed separation recovers 7.2 g grain and 7.2 g stones, conserving 14.4 g. Purity and recovery measure different things. Handpicking needs visible distinguishable pieces and cannot remove dissolved material.

Precautions and limitations

Use dry, visible, easily distinguished pieces. Do not taste laboratory samples. Handpicking is unsuitable for dissolved substances, very fine mixtures or visually indistinguishable particles. Chosen object masses are not measurements of real rice or stones. Motion is a controlled transfer, not a solved granular/contact simulation. No food-safety certification is implied. Internal engineering review and editorial class placement are separate from independent scientific and board verification.
