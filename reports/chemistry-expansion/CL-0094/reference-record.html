Turmeric reference wells · practical record

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited

Aim

Compare acid,base and water indicator references and account for a finite base-then-acid investigation.

Student hypothesis and plan

Track separate wells and equivalent amounts.

Apparatus

Four independent wells with prepared trace-indicator references,selected dilute strong-reference stocks,metered applicators and an observation notebook.

Procedure

  1. Aim: compare prepared turmeric-indicator reference wells and investigate whether a selected acid dose changes a previously basic investigation well back toward yellow.
  2. Use an explicitly prepared trace-indicator reference in four separate wells. The water,acid-dose and base-dose controls are already prepared independently. The fourth well receives the staged additions. Do not claim that arbitrary turmeric powder dissolves completely in water.
  3. Record initial aqueous volume per well,chosen strong-reference acid/base concentrations,planned aliquot volumes and actual delivered fractions. Each well has its own liquid inventory;the three controls do not draw water from the investigation well.
  4. Inspect the water and acid-dose controls. Both may be yellow,so yellow alone cannot distinguish an acid from a neutral reference. Record the base-dose control too;zero or dilute base may not show the chosen rust-red endpoint.
  5. Meter the selected base aliquot into the investigation well. Record volume,base equivalents and the current endpoint. Staged delivery time is not a measured chemical rate.
  6. Add the chosen acid reference. Track both delivered equivalent amounts. Use one acid equivalent per base equivalent for the selected ideal HCl/NaOH reference reaction.
  7. Calculate neutralized formal equivalents as the smaller delivered amount. Keep excess acid/base equivalent amounts separate from free H⁺/OH⁻ concentrations,which include water autoionization.
  8. Calculate total additive solution volume,signed excess concentration and optional ideal25°CpH. These are model calculations,not values measured from turmeric hue. Include water equilibrium instead of setting a zero excess concentration equal to zero H⁺.
  9. Compare the selected acid capacity with the full planned base dose. If acid is insufficient,retain the calculated basic outcome. If there is no base dose,the preparation does not demonstrate a base-to-acid reversal.
  10. Write the observed endpoint and contrasting evidence. Our conservative unresolved basic region is not an exact measured transition range. Yellow is not proof of exact neutralization. State the trace-indicator,isothermal,instant-mixing and uncalibrated-colour assumptions.
