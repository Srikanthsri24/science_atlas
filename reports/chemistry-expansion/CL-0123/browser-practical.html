Metal displacement: reactivity and finite stocks

VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0123

Aim

Compare selected Zn/Fe/Cu divalent sulfate displacement cases using finite inventories, no-net-reaction controls and conserved element/charge accounts.

Apparatus

Virtual metal coupons, selected sulfate reference solutions, volume and mass inputs, progress control and species-account visualizer.

Safety

Virtual only. Real metal/salt-solution practicals require teacher supervision, suitable protective equipment and approved chemical-waste handling. Do not infer drinking-water safety from this model.

Prediction and plan

Compare Zn, Fe and Cu; hold volume and concentration constant. Record original metal and deposit separately.

Procedure

  1. Predict the selected activity order and write the expected divalent displacement equation. Compare only the offered Zn, Fe and Cu sulfate cases.
  2. Record coupon identity/mass, target-ion identity/concentration and solution volume. Equal coupon mass does not mean equal initial moles.
  3. Bring the coupon into contact, then advance the controlled reaction extent. The animation stages stoichiometry; its seconds are not measured laboratory reaction time.
  4. Pause and record original coupon remaining, deposited metal and dissolved species separately. Identify the potential limiting stock before interpreting the endpoint.
  5. Test same-metal, reverse-order, no-contact and zero-stock controls. No net selected displacement does not rule out microscopic exchange or excluded corrosion chemistry.
  6. Use volume in litres, n=m/M and n=cV. Apply the1:1metal/ion stoichiometry and two-electron transfer, bounded by finite inventories.
  7. Check each element across both solid and bath, and include the sulfate spectator in the charge account. Treat colour as a schematic aid, not a concentration measurement.
  8. Write an exam-style result with substituted calculations, conclusion and limitations. State that passivation, competing water/acid/oxygen reactions and kinetics are excluded.
