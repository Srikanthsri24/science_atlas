## Model and original heat-balance derivation

Constant cp4.18J/(gK), source-vessel heat capacity40J/K. Before boiling C0=m0cp+Cv; C0 dT/dt=P−UA(T−20). For UA>0, T=20+(P/UA)(1−exp(−UA t/C0)); for UA0, T=20+Pt/C0. Finite boiling is possible only if P>UA*80. Otherwise boiling time/target time are null, never false finite values or Infinity. If possible, tb=−C0/UA ln(1−UA*80/P), or C0*80/P for insulation.

During boiling at100°C, net power Pb=P−UA*80; vapour rate=Pb/2256.4g/s. Total evaporation is capped at m0−10. Heater cuts off at tc=tb+(m0−10)/rate. Afterwards remaining water/vessel cool exponentially to20°C with heat capacity10cp+Cv and the same chosen UA; UA0 is an insulated hold. No dry heating, forced evaporation beyond inventory or source mass creation.

The external coolant stream remains separate from feed/condensate. Its ideal quasi-steady latent capacity is mdot*cp*(35−Tin)W, with35°C a chosen outlet design limit. Capture fraction=min(1,Qcapacity/Pb). Condensed mass=capture*evaporated; remainder is escaped feed water. Condensate is saturated liquid at100°C in an ideal insulated collector: latent heat is removed, subcooling is NOT modelled. Do not show cool collected water without adding an explicit sensible-cooling energy balance. Empty collector temperature is null. Pump operates only while the source boils; throughput=mdot*active-boiling-time. Current outlet temperature is null when flow stops; cumulative mean outlet temperature may remain available. Quasi-steady zero hold-up and negligible exchanger thermal capacity are approximations; conduit/marker overlays are not extra feed or coolant inventories.

Primary-feed mass: remaining+recovered+escaped=m0. Coolant throughput is recorded separately, not counted as recovered feed. The conditional collection-minus-discarded-coolant metric is an operational comparison if coolant is discarded, not water destruction or a closed mass balance. Returning/recycling coolant changes that interpretation. Supplied heat is delivered thermal input P*tactive; no electrical efficiency or pump-energy claim.

Constant-pressure reference enthalpy/heat account relative to20°C: source sensible=(mremaining cp+Cv)(T−20), recovered sensible=mrecovered cp*80, escaped enthalpy=mescaped(cp*80+L), coolant heat=mrecovered L. Ambient loss=warm input−C0(Twarm−20)+UA*80*tboilactive+(10cp+Cv)(100−Taftercut). Their sum equals delivered heat at every stage. Latent enthalpy includes vaporization at the reference pressure; this is a consistent simplified heat/enthalpy budget, not an exact full EOS/internal-energy/pV calculation. All terms use grams, seconds, watts, joules, kelvin/Celsius differences.

Target time=tb+target/recoveryRate only when target does not exceed capture-limited maximum. Distinguish future target prediction from current measured recovered mass and whether that target lies inside the2400s run. No-cooling/no-boiling/undersized-cooling controls must not claim a successful target. More heater power can shorten warmup but overwhelm the same condenser and decrease final capture. More once-through cooling can recover more feed while using far more external cooling water; compare heat per gram AND coolant per gram.

Liquid-volume reference linearly interpolates saturated-liquid densities.99816g/cm³ at20°C and.95835 at100°C. This is a chosen approximation ignoring pressure dependence/actual nonlinear expansion, not a full IAPWS implementation. Source volume follows its mass and model temperature; collected volume uses hot saturated-liquid density. No visible opaque steam volume or arbitrary bubble inventory should be added. Escaped water is invisible vapour; moving directional cues must be labelled explanatory.
