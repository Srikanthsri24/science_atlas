Separate iron filings from sand magnetically

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0040 · Class4 editorial investigation

Aim

Compare magnetic pickup at equal gaps and physical times. Account for iron remaining at the source, rising and captured, and distinguish product purity from iron recovery.

Apparatus and principle

Virtual fixed magnetic pickup face, shallow mixed reference sample, adjustable local-axis field and gap. Twenty-four enlarged iron markers each represent1000 independent reference kernels, not clumps. Initial iron12g and pure quartz sand36g. A reversible induced moment in a field gradient experiences an upward force. A kernel begins to rise only if that force exceeds its weight.

Prediction

Iron should rise when the upward magnetic force exceeds its weight. Compare equal-gap fields and both poles; pure quartz stays in the source in this ideal model.

Procedure

  1. Record a magnet-off reference at the same pickup gap.
  2. Enable the reference field and observe the initial lift test for a selected kernel.
  3. Pause at equal physical times. Record source, rising and captured iron separately, together with source sand.
  4. At0.3s, check whether any kernel remains airborne before describing a settled result.
  5. Repeat with a different field at the same gap; separately compare gaps at the same field. Reverse the pole as a control.
  6. Calculate recovery and purity. Support the result with a mass balance and state a limitation.
