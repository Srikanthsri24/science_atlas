Sublimation as separation

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited

Aim,hypothesis and plan

Compare collected iodine recovery with source residue purity. Retain source,vapour and deposit inventories at onset,exhaustion and equal physical clocks. Change one preparation variable at a time.

Apparatus and reference preparation

A closed virtual chamber with an iodine/inert-grain source,controlled source and collector surfaces and a stated free gas volume. Surface temperatures remain within the solid-state reference range of 40–80°C. The model is not a physical handling procedure.

Procedure and observation instructions

  1. Aim: investigate solid-to-vapour transfer and solid deposition from a mixture of iodine and an inert nonvolatile grain reference. Predict how collector temperature changes recovery.
  2. Record initial masses,source and collector temperatures,exposed areas and free gas volume. The reference thermostats are already at their selected temperatures;no warm-up ramp is simulated.
  3. Keep the virtual chamber closed. Track source iodine,vapour iodine and collected solid separately. The carrier gas starts at atmospheric pressure;zero initial iodine partial pressure does not mean a vacuum.
  4. Record the onset of deposition when iodine partial pressure reaches the collector equilibrium pressure. A warm or covered collector may collect nothing.
  5. Repeat at the same physical clock while changing one preparation parameter. Parameter changes start a fresh preparation;they do not simulate reheating an existing deposit.
  6. Calculate component balances,iodine recovery and source residue mass purity. Empty collection has no established deposit purity. The ideal model omits entrainment and other depositing substances.
  7. Record the reference latent-heat budget separately from a full energy balance. Sensible heat,glass heating and thermostat losses are omitted.
  8. Write a result with measured model values and a conclusion restricted to the reference assumptions. Report residual vapour or source iodine instead of claiming complete recovery.
