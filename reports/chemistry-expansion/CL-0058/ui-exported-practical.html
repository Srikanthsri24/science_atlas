Investigate simple distillation of coloured water

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0058 · Class 5 editorial investigation

Aim

Follow heating, vaporisation, condensation and collection. Account for water in every compartment and trace nonvolatile dye in the residue.

Apparatus and model

Virtual heated flask, stillhead, inner vapour passage, separate cooling jacket and open receiver. Cooling water enters the lower jacket port and leaves the upper port. It never mixes with the distillate. Net heating stops at half the initial water; the model does not boil the pot dry. Temperature shown is a pot reference, not a stillhead sensor reading.

Prediction

Water should collect after warming, boiling and finite transport delays. Nonvolatile dye should remain in the pot, and limited cooling should reduce recovery. Include vented water in the budget. <check> & "units"

Procedure

  1. Record water, dye, starting temperature, net heater power, coolant flow and inlet temperature.
  2. Observe the separated vapour and cooling paths and the open receiver. Predict dye retention and water recovery.
  3. Run the guide or manual investigation. Pause before boiling, at first arrival, during collection, at the heater cutoff and after the last drop.
  4. Record pot, vapour transit, drain, receiver and vented water. Include the external vent inventory in the budget.
  5. Compare matched heater settings with effective, limited and zero cooling. Distinguish evaporation from collection.
  6. Substitute values with units; calculate heat, condenser capacity, recovery and retained dye concentration. State the result and assumptions.
