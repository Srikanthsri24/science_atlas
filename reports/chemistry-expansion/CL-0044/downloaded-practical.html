Separate a two-colour ink by paper chromatography

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0044 · Class 4 editorial investigation

Aim

Observe solvent rise and dye migration, measure all distances from one pencil origin, and compare separation with an equal-affinity control.

Apparatus and model

Virtual paper strip,development chamber,constant-level solvent pool,pencil origin and rulers. The origin is 2 cm above the lower paper edge and 1.2 cm above the pool. Reference ink contains 1 mg each of blue and yellow virtual dyes. The chosen solvent-front law is0.8+sqrt(ct)cm; chosen dye distribution ratios use equal reference phase volumes. These are not measured commercial ink or solvent values.

Prediction

The lower-affinity dye should travel farther from the pencil origin. Equal affinities should overlap.

Procedure

  1. Prepare a small mixed reference spot on the pencil origin, above the solvent pool. Record the dry initial state.
  2. Begin a fresh run and watch the solvent rise. Note when it reaches the origin.
  3. Pause at a chosen physical time. Record solvent-front distance and each dye peak distance from the same origin.
  4. Apply Rf=dye distance/front distance. Leave it undefined before resolved front travel.
  5. Repeat with equal affinities as a co-elution control, then reverse affinities. Keep other settings fixed.
  6. Record widths and nominal resolution as teacher extensions. Account for both 1 mg dyes without treating colour intensity as a mass measurement.
  7. Write a supported result and conclusion. One overlapping band does not prove that only one dye is present.

Result and conclusion

The reference yellow dye travelled farther than blue because its chosen stationary-phase affinity was lower. Both Rf values used distances from the same pencil origin. Equal-affinity overlap did not establish purity. These results apply to the stated virtual-dye model, not calibrated commercial inks.

Precautions and limitations

Use one pencil origin above the pool,mark the solvent front and compare matched fresh runs. The reference capillary law omits gravity,evaporation and solvent depletion. Mobilisation is instantaneous at origin wetting. Equal phase volumes and constant distribution ratios are assumptions. Wet-domain-truncated Gaussian profiles and prescribed broadening are phenomenological,not a solved transport or adsorption-kinetics PDE. Peak/mode position is the migration reference,not a truncated centroid. Nominal Gaussian resolution is not measured purity. Colour and transverse shape are illustrative. No real ink identity,method calibration,independent expert or official-board certification is claimed.
