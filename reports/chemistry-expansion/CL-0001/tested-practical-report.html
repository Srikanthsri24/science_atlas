Practical record: Sort objects by their material

Class 1 · CL-0001 · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited

Aim

Group six selected objects by the material they are made from.

Apparatus and samples

Six virtual sorting trays; spoon, block, bottle, marble, eraser and pebble.

Principle

Material is what an object is made from; shape and use are different properties. These samples have specified materials. Appearance alone cannot identify all real materials.

Prediction

The six selected samples can be grouped by what they are made from.

Procedure

  1. Inspect a sample and read its description.
  2. Choose its material group.
  3. Record the result and describe the evidence.
  4. Repeat with all six samples and compare the groups.

Model limits

This is a classification activity using known virtual samples and their specified main materials. Objects may contain several materials. This model does not measure hardness, composition, safety or chemical reactivity, and objects with the same name may be made from different materials.
