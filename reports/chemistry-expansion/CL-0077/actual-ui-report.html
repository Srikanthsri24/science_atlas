CL-0077 · Density from mass and displaced volume

Aim,apparatus and procedure

Zeroed reference balance,calibrated cylinder,insoluble ellipsoid specimen and negligible-volume holder. Reporting increments are explicit.

  1. Aim: determine specimen density from a dry mass reading and the difference of two calibrated water-volume readings.
  2. Use the zeroed reference balance. Record dry mass and its display increment before the specimen enters water.
  3. Record the initial liquid reading at the central bottom-of-meniscus reference. The fixed wall profile is included in the scale calibration.
  4. Lower the insoluble specimen without bubbles or contact with the floor or walls. Record a partial-immersion trial as a comparison,not a valid density determination.
  5. Fully submerge the specimen. Record the final water reading using the same display increment. Do not mistake the final total reading for specimen volume.
  6. Subtract final minus initial readings. Divide dry mass by this difference. Convert g/mL to kg/m³ by multiplying by1000.
  7. For the teacher extension,calculate worst-case rounding bounds. These exclude calibration,wetting,bubbles and other physical errors and are not confidence intervals.
  8. Write the result with units,compare partial and complete immersion,and conclude using the observation table.

Prediction and plan

Weigh dry,record initial water,compare partial and full immersion,then test reporting increments.
