Investigate gas occupying space in an inverted cup

CL-0067 · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · Virtual practical

Aim

Compare vertical trapped-air and atmospheric-vent cups in matched finite basins. Observe water entry, retained air, hydrostatic pressure and contact history during immersion and withdrawal.

Principle and apparatus

Isothermal ideal-air reference, hydrostatic water pressure and conserved finite basin water. Inverted transparent cups, water basins, vertical motion carriage, one ideal atmospheric vent ending above the water, diagnostic scales and a zero-volume contact marker beneath each roof. The marker is a contact proxy, not a paper-wicking or drying simulation.

Procedure

Record cup and basin dimensions, atmosphere, temperature and planned insertion. Predict the two outcomes. Record both initial references. Lower, hold and withdraw vertically; pause at matched times and note actual depth, outside water rise, inside water and retained air. Compare shallow, deeper and zero-insertion preparations. Record gas pressure as not applicable when the vented cavity is flooded; do not substitute water pressure. Apply the worked formulas with units, verify water and air inventories, and write result, conclusion and limitations. Keep observed marker history after withdrawal.

Air occupies space; compare both preparations. <angle> & reasoning.

Result, conclusion and limitations

Trapped air compresses and expands; vented air leaves then returns. Water is conserved.

Vertical quasi-static motion, flat water interfaces, incompressible water and uniform isothermal dry ideal air. No sloshing, inertia, capillarity, meniscus, gas dissolution, vapour inventory, thermal transients, gas-head gradient, leaks, finite vent resistance/dead volume, real paper absorption or drying solver. Atmospheric vent and contact marker have zero modeled displacement. Camera orbit is not physical cup tilt. Any gas/flow tint is an explanatory overlay; real air is clear. Class placement is editorial; quantitative pressure and inventory calculations are teacher extensions.
