Aim,hypothesis and plan

Aim:generate and store gas before testing independent limewater. Record source,syringe,receiver & bypass separately. Literal notation:<gas> and "solid".

Apparatus and procedure

  1. Aim:generate gas from a finite acid/carbonate source,transfer a controlled aliquot to independent limewater,and interpret clouding or clearing with separate material accounts.
  2. Apparatus:prepared reaction vessel,closed dry pre-purged gas path,metered gas syringe,independent limewater receiver and explicit bypass outlet. This virtual setup is not a home mixing instruction.
  3. Record initial carbonate mass,acid volume/concentration and limewater volume/concentration. State nominal drawing densities,dry gas conditions and the sequential teaching-envelope assumptions.
  4. Observe the zero-generation control. Generate gas before transfer;record reacted source amount,remaining carbonate/acid and actual gas stored in the syringe. Gas in a syringe has not yet tested limewater.
  5. Transfer gas in recorded stages. The transfer fraction refers to maximum generatable gas,not to currently stored gas. Never transfer more than has been generated. Use a fresh preparation before changing reactant quantities.
  6. Record white precipitate,partial redissolution or a cleared-envelope state,alongside remaining lime,solid carbonate,dissolved bicarbonate and gas bypass. A clear receiver with no reagent is a different negative control.
  7. Convert grams and millilitres to moles using the stated molar masses and concentrations. Apply CaCO₃+2HCl→CaCl₂+CO₂+H₂O;account for limiting reagent and source reaction water.
  8. Use nRT/P only for the explicitly dry ideal gas conditions. Separate generated,remaining syringe,transferred,consumed and bypass gas;do not count one parcel in two locations.
  9. For the sequential limit envelope,calculate precipitation and redissolution extents separately. Include receiver reaction water and dissolved products. Compare nominal mass-derived fills with material totals;total phase volume is not conserved.
  10. Write formula,substitution,units and result for each relevant calculation. Optional advanced closure checks retain total chemical-contents mass and Ca,C,Cl,H,O atoms across both source and receiver. Hardware tare is excluded from these chemical-content totals.
  11. Write a result and conclusion separately:describe what changed,which controls differed,and why no turbidity,rate,pH or gas-purity calibration was measured. Clearing can follow sufficient excess CO₂;it does not mean no gas was generated.
