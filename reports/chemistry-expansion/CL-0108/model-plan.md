# CL-0108 — Investigate a protective zinc coating

Status: design in progress; no implemented or reviewed lab is claimed. Class 7 placement is editorial and official board mapping remains pending.

## Investigation

Compare separate matched iron specimens: bare, intact zinc coating, scratched coating with conducting contact, and a scratched specimen with an electrically isolated zinc piece. Distinguish a barrier from sacrificial electron supply. Show the aqueous electrolyte return path separately from electron movement in metal; dry air alone is not an electrolyte.

Use a finite-metal, explicitly selected current-budget reference. Do not infer real corrosion speed or service life from standard electrode potentials. Protection must not be asserted for arbitrary damage: accessible zinc area, exposed iron area, electrical contact and a supplied capacity limit matter. AGA field guidance explicitly notes possible failure for large damaged areas.

## Proposed numerical scope requiring independent validation

A selected iron oxidation demand and selected available zinc current limit define an idealized comparison, rather than calibrated electrode kinetics. Zinc supplies up to the selected protective demand when conducting contact, electrolyte and an oxygen-reduction path exist. Any unmet demand remains an iron-conversion reference. Include separate zinc self-consumption, finite zinc exhaustion and subsequent exposure of formerly coated iron. No negative inventories or continued current after all relevant metal is exhausted. Explicitly label abrupt complete-coating-exhaustion exposure as an idealized event, not predicted crack growth.

Track primary M→M²⁺ electron equivalents with Q=2Fn. Oxygen reduction uses four electron equivalents per O₂ and a net water/hydroxide account. If using M(OH)₂ material equivalents, label them accounting products rather than unique actual rust or predicted precipitation. Do not silently mix the prior lab’s mature FeIII oxygen requirement into an FeII-only account. Any phase/ion/product inventory must close Fe, Zn, O, H and charge independently. A prepared external supply is an open boundary, never a falsely sealed mass balance. Numerical input domains and all constants remain to be specified and checked before implementation.

## Visual and practical requirements

Original realistic coated-coupon geometry, disclosed scratch dimensions, exact remaining-metal volumes and a clearly enlarged coating/thickness inspection. Both viewers must distinguish blocked contact, electron travel in metal, ionic return path and zinc exhaustion. Use a 120-second staged guide with at least 60 seconds of meaningful evolving reference states. Surface illustrations must not masquerade as measured coverage or actual microscopic grains.

Notebook: initial preparation, matched-time observations, coated/scratched/disconnected controls, zinc used, iron converted/avoided, primary charge, oxygen/net water supplied, metal/product accounts, formulas with substitution and units, result, limited conclusion and exam-style report.

## Required verification

Independent high-precision reference across preparation corners and event times immediately before/at/after zinc and iron exhaustion. Test zero electrolyte/oxygen/current/contact, zero/complete coating and controlled wide defects; unmet protection demand must remain visible. Reject deliberately corrupted charge, oxygen, unit and exhaustion accounts. Actual exported reports must be validated independently. Exercise both complete guides, every inspection scope, both formats, normal/theatre layouts, real native gestures and saved-record persistence. Keep all review flags pending until evidence exists.
