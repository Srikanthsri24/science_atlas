Supported airspace1–3L, pool/bulk20–40°C, lid10–40°C. Let Ms be the vapour mass at pool saturation and Ml the bulk-temperature-equivalent vapour mass at the lid saturation pressure. a=b=hA/V. Evaporation E=a(Ms−v), condensation C=b max(0,v−Ml), and return D=L/τ with chosen drain timeτ20–120s. Equations:dv/dt=E−C; dL/dt=C−D; dPool/dt=−E+D. Before condensation onset:v=Ms[1−exp(−at)], L=0. When Ms>Ml, onset=−ln(1−Ml/Ms)/a. After onset with x=t−onset, λ=a+b and veq=(aMs+bMl)/λ, v=veq+(Ml−veq)exp(−λx), and C=Csteady[1−exp(−λx)], Csteady=b(veq−Ml). Integrated condensate=Csteady[x−(1−exp(−λx))/λ]. Lid storage solves a first-order driven reservoir; expm1 forms and an equal-decay limit handleλ=1/τ without division by zero. Returned mass=integrated condensate−lid storage, and gross evaporated mass=vapour+integrated condensate. Pool=100−vapour−lid. This preserves both inventories and cumulative-transfer identities. Nonnegative rounding guards act only at floating-point scale.

Reference latent heat2450J/g gives cumulative evaporation input and condensation removal separately. They are not forced equal while vapour storage changes. No total sensible-energy solution, CFD, actual droplet nucleation/shape/coalescence or drain hydrodynamics is claimed. The collecting-lid/drain demonstration labels storage versus cumulative transfer and avoids exaggerating pool depletion. A warm-lid control has no condensation or return; vapour approaches pool saturation.
