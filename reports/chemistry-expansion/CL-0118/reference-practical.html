Ionic and molecular arrangements practical

CL-0118 · Class8 · Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited

Aim and prediction

Compare periodic ionic contents and whole molecular groups.

Materials and method

Virtual periodic rock-salt reference, normalized conventional-cell box, selectable boundary sites, 2D cross-section, 3D inspection and a separate molecular comparison rack. This is a structural-model investigation, not a physical material preparation.

  1. State the aim: compare a periodic ionic reference with a separate collection of whole covalent molecules. Predict ion ratios and molecule atom counts.
  2. Select the number of conventional cells per edge and the molecular reference/count. Record the selected ionic site and the view settings.
  3. Inspect a 2D z-plane and the full 3D box. A cross-section is a viewing subset, not the complete crystal contents. Layer opening is an inspection aid, not physical cleavage.
  4. Separate raw displayed boundary copies from weighted cell contents. Multiply each boundary-class count by its sharing weight and sum each ionic species.
  5. Calculate the Na:Cl ratio, formula units and weighted charge. Explain why the unweighted copy charge is not the charge of an isolated physical crystal.
  6. Select a boundary and an interior site. Count displayed and beyond-box neighbour images; confirm full sixfold bulk coordination and normalized reference distance.
  7. Count whole molecules and atoms per element. Keep intramolecular covalent links inside each group; do not invent isolated NaCl molecules in the extended lattice.
  8. Pause the guide to record a labelled observation. Write the result and conclusion using the tables and substitutions, with the model limitations.

Result and conclusion

Weights count cell contents. Coordination images continue beyond the display box; molecular groups remain distinct.

Precautions and limitations

  1. Periodic rock-salt reference boxes, not isolated finite crystals. Boundary copies represent shared lattice sites. Weights count cell contents; a fractional contribution is not a fraction of a physical ion.
  2. Whole-molecule positions are a comparison rack, not measured crystal packing or a phase prediction. This does not imply all covalent materials consist of discrete molecules.
  3. Cell edge is normalized to1. Display ionic radii, molecular lengths, layer-opening distances, scan speeds and animation timing are arbitrary inspection choices. No measured distances or material-property predictions are produced.
  4. Bulk coordination includes explicit beyond-box periodic images. In a one-cell repeat, distinct neighbour images can share a periodic representative ID. Dashed neighbour links are coordination annotations, not covalent bonds.
  5. A 2D plane omits out-of-plane neighbours. Full-box weighted contents remain unchanged when a slice or layer separation is selected.
  6. No ionization process, chemical reaction, force solver, energy, melting point, solubility or conductivity is simulated.
