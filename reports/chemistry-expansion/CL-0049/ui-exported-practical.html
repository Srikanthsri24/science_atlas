Observe melting and freezing of a chosen material

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0049 · Class 4 editorial investigation

Aim

Use reference pure water to distinguish temperature change from melting/freezing. Observe constant-temperature phase changes and conserve sample mass and transferred heat.

Apparatus and model

Original virtual 250 mL receiver,reference ice/water,ideal thermometer and thermal controller. Constant representative values: cice = 2090 J/(kg K),cwater = 4186 J/(kg K),Lf = 334000 J/kg. The energy reference is solid ice at 0°C. The controller specifies net heat into/out of the sample,not electrical efficiency,and cuts heat flow at −20°C/60°C. The prescribed cube represents phase inventory,not crystal morphology.

Prediction

I predict temperature will pause at0°C during phase change while ice/water masses change. The total mass should stay fixed and net heat should equal supplied minus removed.

Procedure

  1. Choose total sample mass,net heat-rate magnitude and heating,cooling or cycle protocol. Heat/cycle begins with −10°C ice;cooling begins with 10°C water.
  2. Start and pause at several times. Record temperature,solid/liquid masses and separately supplied/removed heat.
  3. Identify the 0°C melting or freezing interval. Check that phase fraction changes while temperature stays fixed.
  4. Compare a single-phase interval,where transferred heat changes temperature,with the latent interval,where it changes phase.
  5. For the cycle,the thermal direction reverses at 60 s. Compare equal supplied/removed heat and the final state.
  6. Repeat with a different mass or heat rate while holding other settings fixed. Record actual delivered heat and any demand suppressed by the controller limits.
  7. Apply the substitutions below. Write a supported result,conclusion and limitation. Optional geometry extension:compare floor support with hydrostatic flotation of the representative ice cube.
