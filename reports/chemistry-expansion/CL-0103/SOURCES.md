# CL-0103 private scientific provenance

Read 2026-11-27. These sources support principles, not the chosen numerical rate constants or an official Class7 board mapping. No source links enter the student UI.

- OpenStax Chemistry §12.4, https://openstax.org/books/chemistry/pages/12-4-integrated-rate-laws : first-order concentration decays exponentially; half-life depends on the rate coefficient. Its peroxide example uses a particular dataset. We use the mathematical law with explicitly assumed independent coefficients, not that dataset or a certified peroxide catalyst calibration.
- Vince Hradil / Triton College, General Chemistry II Lab Manual §10, https://chem.libretexts.org/Courses/Triton_College/General_Chemistry_II_Lab_Manual/10:_Decomposition_of_Hydrogen_Peroxide : balanced peroxide decomposition yields water and oxygen. Actual kinetics depend on conditions and must be measured. The manual observes gas pressure; our original model uses freely expanding gas syringes instead.
- OpenStax Chemistry2e §9.2, https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/9-2-relating-pressure-volume-amount-and-temperature-the-ideal-gas-law : PV=nRT with compatible kelvin, kPa and litre units; selected rounded R=8.314 kPa L mol−1 K−1. Constant pressure and gas temperature give incremental volume proportional to generated gas amount.

All explanations, geometry, calculations and instructional sequences are original. Molecular masses use internally consistent teaching atomic masses H1.008,O15.999 g/mol; initial air is approximated as28.97g/mol. Model omissions and constants remain explicit in the practical report.

- NASA Science, https://science.nasa.gov/earth/climate-change/greenhouse-gases/the-atmosphere-getting-a-handle-on-carbon-dioxide/ : dry air contains approximately20.95% oxygen by volume. The model therefore includes original oxygen when displaying total gas oxygen fraction; generated oxygen remains a separate reaction quantity.
