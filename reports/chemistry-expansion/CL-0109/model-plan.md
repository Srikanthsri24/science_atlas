# CL-0109 — Compare water hardness using a soap-foam model

Status: connected model, notebook and both viewers; browser and numerical evidence recorded. Native camera gestures and final frozen review remain pending. Class 7 is editorial placement; official mapping remains pending.

Compare four matched preparations: calcium/magnesium-free control, calcium only, magnesium only and their mixture. Students choose sample volume, calcium and magnesium concentration, soap-stock concentration, finite delivered soap volume and agitation. Show a common preparation and delivery sequence followed by matched shaking and an explicit qualitative foam comparison. Aim for a 120-second guided sequence with at least 60 seconds of observable delivery/mixing/agitation, not a decorative loop or a measured precipitation-rate claim.

The idealized reaction is M²⁺ + 2 RCOO⁻ → M(RCOO)₂ equivalents. Track calcium, magnesium, free soap, soap-bound divalent ions and sodium/chloride counterions. Finite soap must be partitioned explicitly; proportional allocation between calcium and magnesium is a chosen assumption, not a solubility-product prediction. Verify this assumption against intended comparisons before implementation.

Calculate initial hardness in mg/L as CaCO₃ from divalent-ion molarity. Report diluted residual hardness separately from initial sample hardness so added soap-stock water does not masquerade as ion removal. Retain finite stock and delivered/remaining portions. Choose and verify a specific soap formula and constants before numerical implementation.

Foam is not a stoichiometric product or a calibrated height measurement. Any excess-soap/agitation indicator must be explicitly labelled a chosen qualitative proxy. Do not claim a measured critical micelle concentration, foam stability, exact bubble size or real-water hardness determination. Air glyphs are visual aids and must not appear as generated gas in the chemical mass account.

Required verification: zero soap and zero hardness limits; limiting-reactant exhaustion; calcium/magnesium separation and mixture; ionic-charge, elemental and material balances; delivered stock accounting; independent high-precision reference; deliberately corrupted-result checks; geometric capacities; actual ≥60-second playback in each viewer; responsive text/canvas bounds; paused-frame consistency; native camera drag/wheel; persisted source-only observations and independently validated report substitutions.

Practical: aim, apparatus, controlled variables, dose-by-dose instructions, four-sample observation table, initial and residual hardness calculations, soap consumption/substitution, excess soap, qualitative lather observation, interpretation and limitations. Do not imply a real unknown sample's hardness is certified from the animation.
