# CL-0073 — Classify a material as element, compound or mixture

In progress; no ready/review claim. Original qualitative particle-evidence investigation, twelve dilute gas reference samples, not a bench instrument identifying an unknown chemical from its appearance. Editorial Class6 placement, not official board mapping. Elemental samples can be monatomic He, diatomic O2 or triatomic O3. Pure water vapour, carbon dioxide and methane are compounds. Mixtures include N2/O2 (not complete real-air composition), He/O2, H2O/CO2, unreacted H2/O2, O2/O3 and H2O/O2. Zero-count components do not count as substances. Mixture endpoints may become a pure element or compound. Two oxygen allotropes coexist as a mixture of elemental substances even though only one atomic number is present. Count chemical identities before element kinds; counting element kinds alone misclassifies this case.

No reaction is modelled. Hydrogen and oxygen do not silently become water. All samples are defined low-pressure gas references in a chosen80–200nm cube at280–340K; not standard-pressure liquid/solid specimens.12–48 individual molecular/atomic entities, integer component counts, not a mole-sized representative population or concentration calibration. Relative atomic masses H1.008,C12.011,N14.007,O15.999,He4.002602 are rounded reference values, ignoring isotopic variation. Molecular mass is the stoichiometric sum. Number fractions, mass fractions and atom fractions are different quantities. Primary identity and atom inventories stay fixed through motion.
