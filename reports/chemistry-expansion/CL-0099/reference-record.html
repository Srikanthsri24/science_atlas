Compare indicators before and after dilution

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0099

Aim and prediction

Compare two indicator control/test pairs while distinguishing conserved amount,changing concentration and qualitative colour family.

Compare equal aliquots

Apparatus and procedure

  1. Prepare four equal aliquots of the same acid or base;two contain methyl orange,two phenolphthalein. Keep one of each as an undiluted control.
  2. Record initial volumes,stock concentration and planned water per test. Inspect the shared reservoir containing twice that water.
  3. Add measured water to each test independently. Leave controls unchanged. Compare at the same horizontal2cm path.
  4. Record actual additions,solution volumes,colour families and notes. Calculate amounts and concentrations. Use computedpH as an ideal model prediction.
  5. Explain whether colour family changed and why a colourless indicator still retains tracer material. Do not infer measured absorbance from this illustration.
