Isothermal constant-area net transfer: dm/dt = k_m A (rho_sat − rho_air), limited by available liquid. Water and surrounding air share a fixed temperature; externally supplied heat maintains it. Ambient humidity is a maintained reservoir. Saturation pressure: 610.8 exp[17.27 T_C/(T_C+237.3)] Pa; saturated vapour density p/(461.5 T_K). Chosen mass transfer coefficient 0.001 + 0.004 × air setting m/s. Reference liquid density 1 g/mL and latent heat 2.45 MJ/kg. Remaining plus transferred mass closes exactly; actual rate is zero once dry. No starting water means no drying-completion percentage. Saturated air at equal temperature has zero net transfer, while microscopic exchange may continue.

Water vapour is invisible. Markers are explicitly schematic net-transfer cues. No visible steam cloud is claimed. Thin films, contact-angle area changes, thermal transients, local saturation and calibrated airflow are excluded. Not a weather or physical drying-time prediction.
