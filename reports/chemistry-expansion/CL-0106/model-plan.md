# CL-0106 — Conditions required for iron corrosion

Status: core account in progress; no review or official board mapping claimed.

Use matched iron specimens in three control stations: oxygen plus water, water with oxygen excluded, and dry oxygen-containing air. A fourth optional salt-water comparison may demonstrate an assumed rate difference, without presenting the rate as measured evidence. A 120-second presentation must explicitly represent a multi-day observation period, not real-time visible corrosion.

Separate the observation experiment from an advanced stoichiometric account. Rust is compositionally variable: do not assign a single exact formula, density, product mass, hydration number, or volumetric expansion to all rust. Any selected hydrated-oxide reference composition must be named as an assumption, with separate oxygen uptake and water uptake accounts and no invented empirical calibration. Better to keep core evidence qualitative and calculate controlled preparation quantities, elapsed time and matched specimen dimensions than invent numerical corrosion measurements.

Controls must explain experimental imperfections: ideal oxygen exclusion and dry air are boundary assumptions, not claims that practical preparation removes every trace. Oil acts as a barrier to replenishment rather than a reagent that reverses rust. Drying-agent granules must be separated from the specimen. Distinguish oxygen consumption, dissolved oxygen, residual headspace gas and water transfer if any finite-inventory quantitative model is adopted.

Visual work: matched rack, readable specimen close-ups, progressive corrosion coverage explicitly marked schematic, moving timeline, oxygen/water availability annotations, paused views, independent 2D and 3D inspection, portrait stacking and clear mobile captions. Do not use flame or instantaneous transformation.

Practical: aim, control-variable table, dated/model-day observations, evidence classification, justified conclusion, limitations, and genuine worked preparation/geometry calculations. Exports retain only source preparations and student writing; recalculate derived fields. Scientific and UI gates remain pending.


## Selected quantitative reference (implemented core)

Use the conditional net account 4Fe + 3O2 + 2xH2O -> 2(Fe2O3.xH2O). The hydration number x is explicitly selected, including x=0 as a dry-oxide accounting limit; it is not inferred from rust colour or claimed universal. Water remains an environmental requirement even when net hydration consumption is zero. Initial water and accessible oxygen are finite. The account includes iron, accessible oxygen and water only; unchanged inert gas, oil, drying agent and apparatus tare are outside that boundary. No final pressure or rust density is calculated.

A selected 1-exp(-k*t) target fraction advances the wet reference, capped by oxygen and hydration-water budgets. Neither k nor the resulting multi-day progression is calibrated experimental data. Dry and oxygen-excluded controls retain their metal. A zero-k control is distinct from reactant exclusion. The guide stages seven illustrative model days over 90 seconds within a 120-second presentation. It must prominently disclose time compression and the chosen law. This does not establish real-world corrosion speed.

The iron geometry includes a 0.3 cm conical tip, cylindrical shaft and a 0.12 cm cylindrical head with twice the shaft radius. Initial and remaining radii preserve the selected pure-iron material volume. A surface highlight may illustrate conversion, but cannot be presented as a quantitatively predicted rust-layer volume. Retention of all product is assumed; flaking, aqueous intermediate inventories, electrolytes, passivation, temperature effects and corrosion potentials are not computed.


Prepared water is restricted to20-40g to keep the supported nail lengths fully covered beneath the oil barrier. The visible liquid levels describe the initial preparation,not a predicted evolving rust displacement. A connected finite oxygen-supply schematic must make clear that the accessible oxygen budget is not all dissolved in the water or inferred from tube pressure. Default chosen k is0.02 per day, keeping conversion active throughout the90-second guide progression; this is an authored reference setting,not calibration.
