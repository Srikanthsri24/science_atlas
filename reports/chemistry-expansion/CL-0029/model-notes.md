# CL-0029 — Dissolving in cooler and warmer water

Internally reviewed; see review.json for date, hashes and scope. Two ideal thermostated cells each hold 10 g sugar and reference 200 mL starting water (approximated as 200 g), with 64 equal cubic grains and reference solid density 1.59 g/cm³. Temperature is held constant by an ideal bath; this is not a heating/cooling transient solver. The model supports 5–60 °C, with a default 10/50 °C comparison. Mixing assumptions remain fixed.

Private reference: American Chemical Society, Middle School Chemistry, Chapter 5 Lesson 6, 2023 teacher PDF, page 6, https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/msc/downloads/chapter-5/lesson-6/ch5-l6-lesson-plan.pdf (accessed 2026-11-25). Its approximate educational sucrose table gives 179, 204, 241 and 288 grams per 100 mL of starting water at 0, 20, 40 and 60 °C respectively. These are not grams per 100 g solution. The code linearly interpolates between those knots, with no extrapolation. Only factual tabulated values are used; explanations, implementation and procedure are original. NOAA CHRIS sucrose sheet supports the reference solid density and the 20 °C solubility anchor; its narrow ambient-temperature table is not extrapolated to warm-water temperatures.

The chosen flux coefficient is k(T)=0.003 exp[(18000/8.314462618)(1/293.15 − 1/(T_C+273.15))] g/(cm² s). The 18000 J/mol sensitivity is an explicit illustrative parameter, not a measured sucrose activation energy or reaction-rate constant. Both the temperature-dependent reference capacity and the chosen transfer coefficient enter the shrinking-grain equation. A coefficient ratio alone is not an exact target-time ratio when capacity also changes.

As in the reviewed reference model, da/dt = −2k/rho [1 − (M0 − rho N a³)/capacity]. Its separated integral is inverted with bisection, and the 9 g target time is computed at a0 × cube-root(0.1). The imposed initial state is exact. Solid plus dissolved sugar remains 10 g and combined sugar remains 20 g. At 20 °C the trajectory agrees with the unchanged reviewed CL-0022 zero-stirring reference. Independent RK4 integrations test the new temperature-dependent solution.

Twelve targeted model, geometry and record tests cover table basis and interpolation, kelvin conversion, conservation, equal/exchanged temperatures, reference-model agreement, independent integration, target crossings, continuous 110-second guide specification and invalid inputs. Water density, solid thermal expansion, volume changes, heat flow, convection, clumping and support contact obstruction are excluded. UI and practical review passed: full 110-second guide and 180-model-second manual trial, sixteen GUI trials, both target transitions, equal/exchanged temperatures, boundary controls, fitted 3D thermometers, grain inspection, five viewport sizes, report download and persisted notes. Target-time substitutions include the separated integral and are independently checked by Simpson quadrature. Fresh-load runtime logs are clean. Full regression 1953 and the chemistry suite passed; production code build and TypeScript passed. Scope is internal engineering/model-consistency review only.
