Compare dissolving in warm and cool water

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · Class 3 · CL-0029

Aim

Compare how maintained water temperature changes the model time to dissolve 9 g of a matched sugar sample.

Apparatus and principle

Two matched cells, ideal temperature-controlled baths, calibrated model thermometers, a common clock and 10 g sugar divided into 64 equal cubic grains in each cell. Each starts with reference 200 mL water, approximated as 200 g. Temperature-sensitive transfer and an approximate temperature-dependent reference capacity determine shrinking-grain dissolution. Sugar remains accounted for after it dissolves. Thermostat maintenance and bulk mixing are ideal assumptions; heat flow and fluid motion are not solved.

Prediction

QA trial: the matched 50 °C sample should reach 9 g dissolved before the 10 °C sample under the chosen temperature-sensitive model.

Procedure

  1. Set A to 10 °C and B to 50 °C. Both samples are pre-equilibrated at their selected temperatures before the release.
  2. Check identical water amount, sugar mass, grain count and mixing assumptions. Record the initial state.
  3. Start both cells together. Pause at the same model times and record temperatures, remaining solid, dissolved mass and whether the common 9 g target has been reached.
  4. Check solid plus dissolved sugar equals 10 g in each cell. A completed cell has no further dissolution rate.
  5. Compare the predicted model target times. Apply T_K = T_C + 273.15 before substituting into the temperature-sensitive coefficient.
  6. Repeat with equal temperatures, then exchange A and B. Write a result supported by your records and one model limitation.
