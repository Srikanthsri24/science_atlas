Diffusion through still water

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0037 · Class 3 editorial investigation

Aim

Observe a concentrated dye spot spreading in a closed, magnified water microcell without stirring.

Apparatus and model

Virtual closed cubical water cell, trace dye reference and model clock. Still-water diffusion only; no injection flow, convection, reaction or calibrated food-dye optics. Effective radius and water viscosity are teaching references.

Prediction

The dark central spot will spread without stirring; total dye will remain in the closed water cell.

Procedure

  1. Choose cell side and effective radius, then record the initial concentrated pattern.
  2. Advance the model clock without changing controls. Pause at several times; note spread and concentration.
  3. Keep dye mass fixed. Repeat with one changed variable and compare equal model times.
  4. Use the finite-cell calculation for late observations; state why the early free-space estimate eventually fails.
