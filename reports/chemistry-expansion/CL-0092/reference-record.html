Aim

Compare repeat weighing readings,zero correction,mean and spread for a fixed specimen.

Student hypothesis and plan

Controlled repeat weighing.

Apparatus

Simulated digital balance,sealed reference specimen,empty-pan check and observation notebook.

Procedure

  1. Aim: repeat the weighing of the same sealed specimen and compare raw and zero-corrected readings,mean and spread.
  2. Record the chosen reference specimen mass,display step,fixed zero offset,loaded-reading noise setting,seed and planned repeat count. The reference mass is known within this simulation only.
  3. With the specimen removed,record the empty indication b₀. This simplified empty check has no random variation. Do not mistake the display step for a complete accuracy specification.
  4. Place the same specimen on the pan and wait for the settled stage. Record the displayed mass in grams. Remove it and repeat without changing the preparation.
  5. For each reading calculate xcorrected=xraw−b₀. Record both raw and corrected values;sub-step zero-offset residuals can remain.
  6. Calculate sum,mean,minimum,maximum and range separately for raw and corrected readings. Show each deviation from its own series mean and its square.
  7. Optional advanced step: for n≥2 calculate sample variance Σ(x−mean)²/(n−1),sample SD s and repeatability-only standard uncertainty of the mean s/√n. For one reading these sample estimates are not defined.
  8. Compare the mean with the known model reference only to inspect this simulation. Repeatability is not accuracy,and a fixed offset is not removed by collecting more readings.
  9. Repeat with one changed setting,keeping the same seed for a matched computational comparison. A particular longer prefix need not have a smaller realized s/√n. No total uncertainty or confidence percentage is inferred.
  10. Write a conclusion including quantization,zero correction and omitted effects. These generated readings are not physical measurements or a certified calibration.
