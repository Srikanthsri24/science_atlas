# CL-0115 — Construct and compare neutral atomic models

Planned, not implemented. Catalogue class 8 is editorial, pending official board mapping.

Build an interactive atom-construction/comparison investigation with explicit proton, neutron and electron counts. Students should count and place particles, establish neutral charge balance, compare chosen isotopes and explain atomic number versus mass number. Numerical measurements here are discrete counts and charge bookkeeping; do not invent an experimental balance reading or call mass number a measured atomic mass.

Need: original2D/3D nucleus and separate shell-occupancy diagrams, clear staged construction lastingatleast60seconds(target120–150), inspection/isolation, accessible labels and practical observation table showingZ,A,p,n,e,netcharge and occupancy. Worked examples include n=A-Z and neutral e=Z with charge substitution. Restrict to explicitly verified configurations and supported isotope choices. Do not silently construct arbitrary supposedly stable nuclei. Nuclear binding energies, sizes and isotope abundance require separate evidence if shown.

Electrons must not be shown as classical planets following known trajectories. Shell rings may serve as labelled occupancy diagrams. If a probability depiction is added, distinguish a validated hydrogenic example from a generic schematic; do not label decorative dots a solved multi-electron wavefunction. Representative nucleus packing is not a measured nuclear geometry. Animation can assemble, highlight and compare counts without suggesting physical nuclear assembly by hand.
