Construct and compare neutral atomic models — practical record

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0115

Aim and prediction

Construct and compare known neutral isotope counting models.

Apparatus and principle

Virtual isotope cards, proton/neutron/electron counting tokens, a schematic nucleus and labelled shell-occupancy slots. Observation means counting the completed model, not measuring nuclear dimensions or atomic mass.

Procedure and recording instructions

  1. Write the aim: construct a selected neutral isotope model and compare it with another known isotope or element. These are counting diagrams, not physical experiments assembling nuclei.
  2. Choose a supported isotope. Record its symbol, atomic number Z and integer mass number A. Predict its proton, neutron and neutral electron counts before placing tokens.
  3. Count and place proton tokens. Apply n = A - Z for neutron tokens. An empty or partly filled diagram is not an identified atom or ion, even when its token charge happens to balance.
  4. Place the required electron tokens in labelled shell-occupancy slots for this selected ground-state reference. Record K, L and M counts. Token-transfer animations and rings do not represent electron trajectories.
  5. Verify p = e for neutrality, A = p + n, and the shell sum equals placed electrons. Convert positive and negative token charges using e0 = 1.602176634 × 10^-19 C; show the substituted values.
  6. Pause after the guide verification stage or complete the model manually before recording. Record both the constructed and comparison references. Compare changes in p, n, e and A.
  7. Write a result and conclusion distinguishing isotopes from different elements. Mass number counts nucleons; it is not an atomic mass in grams or u. Same element does not mean same isotope.

Result and conclusion

Distinguish atomic number, mass number and charge without interpreting the diagram as electron paths or physical nuclear assembly.

Precautions and interpretation limits

  • Restricted to selected known isotope choices of H through Ar and their neutral ground-state shell occupancy. Isotope abundance, atomic mass, stability, nuclear binding and dimensions are not simulated.
  • Partial tokens are an unfinished counting diagram, not an experimentally produced ion, excited atom or nucleus. The comparison always uses completed neutral reference targets.
  • Nuclear packing, shell radii, token colours and transfer arcs are arbitrary display choices. Shell rings are occupancy guides, not classical electron paths. No electron position measurement or multi-electron wavefunction is computed.
  • Saved practical observations require a complete neutral construction. Charge values use the exact elementary-charge constant but are represented numerically in floating point; token and nucleon counts are exact integers.
  • The 150-second sequence explains construction and verification. Its timing is not a physical nuclear assembly process or an electron speed. Class placement is editorial, pending official board mapping.
