# CL-0017 — Compare water and cooking oil pouring

This original investigation compares separate reference liquids draining through matched, primed horizontal outlets. It is not a prediction for arbitrary tilted beakers. Each reservoir has area 20 cm², with a separate collector and a falling source head.

Chosen approximate 20 °C references are water density 1000 kg/m³ with dynamic viscosity 0.001 Pa·s, and oil density 850 kg/m³ with viscosity 0.06 Pa·s. Real oils vary with temperature and composition. Density and viscosity both enter the result; the entire difference is not attributed solely to viscosity.

Controls are initial volume 50–200 mL each, tube radius 0.4–0.8 mm, tube length 20–40 cm and time 0–3600 s. Each source and collector holds at least 240 mL. The full control range stays below Re = 1000. The ideal model assumes fully developed, quasi-steady laminar flow, ignoring entrance losses, surface tension, capillary pinning, outlet menisci, evaporation, temperature changes, tube hold-up and resolved stream transit volume.

With SI units, h = V/A; Δp = ρgh; Q = πr⁴Δp/(8μL). Combining Q with dV/dt = −Q gives V(t) = V₀ exp(−kt), where k = πr⁴ρg/(8μLA). Collected volume is initial minus remaining. Mean speed is Q/(πr²), and Re = ρu(2r)/μ. Each liquid's source-plus-collector volume and tared liquid mass are conserved.

The finite-time exponential keeps source volume positive. “Nearly drained” means less than 1 mL; the model never imposes zero. Values below 0.001 mL are shown as less than 0.001 rather than rounded to an apparent exact zero in the diagrams. Numerical tables retain nine decimal places. Doubling radius raises the initial rate sixteenfold; doubling length halves it.

The 2D display has schematic tube length and enlarged bore. The 3D source and collector cylinders use the stated constant area; their volumes change with calculated head and collection. Sources sit on supported stands, with horizontal outlets above the open collector rims so they discharge to air. 3D tube length follows the selected length, but the bore is enlarged for visibility. Markers indicate flow, not literal droplet counts or particle velocities. Colour aids identification. Comparison mode can be framed as both rigs or either individual liquid; framing does not change the paired experiment.

The guide lasts 110 seconds and compares radii 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8 mm. Each comparison compresses 3600 model seconds into 20 presentation seconds (180×). Manual playback compresses 3600 model seconds into 30 presentation seconds (120×). Both speeds are stated in the interface.
