Grow crystals from a supersaturated solution

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0059 · Class 5 editorial investigation

Aim

Compare seeded growth, chosen unseeded induction and seed dissolution in an undersaturated prepared solution. Account for dissolved salt, crystal salt and water.

Apparatus and model

Virtual covered potassium-nitrate solution held at 20–40°C. Initial seed is already present after cooling and contributes to total salt. The hot preparation and cooling ramp are not animated or thermally simulated. Reference solubility uses a coarse interpolation31.6–63.9g per 100 g water at 20–40°C. Chosen first-order mass relaxation and induction delay are not measured natural kinetics.

Prediction

A supersaturated prepared sample should transfer dissolved salt into solid after activation. A seed contributes mass and a growth surface. An undersaturated sample should dissolve the seed. Compare new solid without pretending A and B have identical total salt. <check> & "units"

Procedure

  1. Record water, initial dissolved charge, seed mass, held temperature, chosen rate and unseeded delay.
  2. Calculate capacity on a water-mass basis. Predict whether the initial solution is above or below it.
  3. Run, pause and record before/after the chosen unseeded activation and during growth. Record new solid separately from the initial seed.
  4. Compare A with an unseeded B using the same dissolved charge. B contains less total salt by A’s seed mass; do not pretend total salt is matched.
  5. Repeat an undersaturated preparation. Observe the seed lose mass rather than grow.
  6. Check dissolved plus solid salt and whole contents. Substitute actual values, state the result and explain assumptions.
