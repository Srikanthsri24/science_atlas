Transparency and light transmission

Science Atlas 360° · VisionariesAI Labs · CL-0002

Aim

Compare transmission and image clarity at equal sample thickness and source power.

Apparatus and principle

Illustrative collimated light source, slit mask, sample holder and screen. Clear samples preserve a directed component; scattering can transmit light without a clear pattern. No lens or geometric image formation is calculated.

Prediction

A clear sample will preserve the pattern; a frosted sample may pass light but scatter it.

Procedure

  1. Set equal thickness and source power.
  2. Compare each sample and record a trial.
  3. Change one variable at a time; record brightness and pattern clarity separately.
  4. Compare evidence, calculate and write your conclusion.

Teacher extension: formulas and worked calculations

P₀: input radiant power (W); Pₜ: transmitted radiant power (W); R: reflected fraction (dimensionless); μ: absorption coefficient (mm⁻¹); d: thickness (mm). Forward scattering coefficient is in mm⁻¹. Powers are model predictions, not sensor readings.

Trial 1: Pₜ = P₀(1 − R)e^(−μd) = 1.00 × (1 − 0.04) × e^(−0.01 × 5.0) = 0.9132 W. Transmission = (1 − 0.04) × e^(−0.01 × 5.0) × 100 = 91.32%. Directed = 0.9132 × e^(−0.001 × 5.0) = 0.9086 W. Diffuse = transmitted − directed = 0.0046 W. Reflected = 1.00 × 0.04 = 0.0400 W. Absorbed = incoming − reflected − transmitted = 0.0468 W. Values are calculated before rounding.

Trial 2: Pₜ = P₀(1 − R)e^(−μd) = 1.00 × (1 − 0.08) × e^(−0.04 × 5.0) = 0.7532 W. Transmission = (1 − 0.08) × e^(−0.04 × 5.0) × 100 = 75.32%. Directed = 0.7532 × e^(−1.2 × 5.0) = 0.0019 W. Diffuse = transmitted − directed = 0.7514 W. Reflected = 1.00 × 0.08 = 0.0800 W. Absorbed = incoming − reflected − transmitted = 0.1668 W. Values are calculated before rounding.

Trial 3: Pₜ = P₀(1 − R)e^(−μd) = 1.00 × (1 − 0.15) × e^(−2 × 5.0) = 0.0000 W. Transmission = (1 − 0.15) × e^(−2 × 5.0) × 100 = 0.00%. Directed = 0.0000 × e^(−0.5 × 5.0) = 0.0000 W. Diffuse = transmitted − directed = 0.0000 W. Reflected = 1.00 × 0.15 = 0.1500 W. Absorbed = incoming − reflected − transmitted = 0.8500 W. Values are calculated before rounding.

Result and conclusion

At equal 5 mm thickness and 1 W input, the clear sample preserved direction, the frosted sample scattered transmitted light and the opaque sample transmitted very little. These chosen coefficients are illustrative, not measured material data.

Precautions and limits

Control thickness and input power. Coefficients are chosen examples, not measured glass or paper data. Forward-scattering slab approximation; no refraction or multiple-scattering solver.
