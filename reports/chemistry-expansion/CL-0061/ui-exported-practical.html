Observe carbon dioxide production from an acid and carbonate

Science Atlas360° · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0061 · Class5 editorial investigation

Aim

Follow carbonate reacting with dilute hydrochloric acid, measure syringe expansion and account for products, remaining reactants and initial trapped air.

Apparatus and principle

Virtual150mL flask, delivery tube with5mL reference dead volume, clamp and200mL free-piston gas syringe. CaCO₃+2HCl→CaCl₂+CO₂+H₂O. The limiting reagent bounds the reaction extent. Quantitative formulas are teacher extensions.

Prediction

Gas yield follows the limiting reagent. Compare piston reading with CO₂ equivalent. <prediction>

Procedure

  1. Record carbonate mass, acid volume/concentration, temperature, pressure and chosen rate.
  2. Predict the limiting reagent. Record the initial clear solution, solid and zero piston reading.
  3. Run, pause and record at regular elapsed times. Compare the shrinking carbonate with the moving piston.
  4. Distinguish generated CO₂-equivalent volume from piston travel volume. The syringe contains an air/CO₂ mixture.
  5. Repeat with excess acid, excess carbonate and a zero-reactant control. Compare finite-time readings with maximum yield.
  6. Substitute values with units. Check material closure and write the result, conclusion and limitations.
