Ionic solid and aqueous solution

VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · CL-0120

Aim

Compare charge transport in ideal solid KCl, an aqueous KCl reference and ideal water.

Apparatus

Virtual low-voltage AC source, paired electrodes, ideal bulk current channel, solid KCl structural inset and reference solution cell.

Safety

Virtual investigation. Do not connect household electricity to liquids. A real practical requires teacher supervision and approved low-voltage equipment.

Plan

Compare solid KCl, solution and water at fixed voltage and geometry.

Procedure

  1. Predict whether charged ions are present in a solid KCl lattice and whether they can translate through it.
  2. Pause the demonstration before recording. Keep paired reference, source voltage and electrode geometry unchanged while comparing materials.
  3. Record the solid control: applied voltage can be present, but ideal fixed-lattice transport current is zero. Write “not measured” for solid conductivity.
  4. Follow separation of existing K+ and Cl− ions and schematic hydration. This visual transition is not a measured rate or a conductivity mixing law.
  5. Select the aqueous KCl reference. Note molality, fixed 25 °C temperature, RMS current and opposite ion drift. Reverse phase and distinguish instantaneous from RMS current.
  6. Compare ideal water at identical geometry and source settings. Retain its small positive current rather than rounding it to zero.
  7. Use the aqueous formulas with SI units. Disconnect the source or lift the electrodes to investigate why inverse readings become unavailable.
  8. Write a result supported by your observations: mobile charged carriers permit solution transport; the mere presence of ions is insufficient in the fixed-lattice model.
