Cabbage indicator: comparing prepared references

CL-0062 · VisionariesAI Labs Private Limited · Virtual practical

Aim

Compare qualitative indicator colours at equal dose in known acid, neutral and base reference buffers. Track liquid and pigment without inferring an unknown sample’s pH.

Apparatus and method

Three equal sample vessels, matched metered dispensers and a virtual timer. Record the settings before dosing. Run, pause and record initial, intermediate and final states for each reference. Compare equal elapsed times and equal doses. Repeat no-dose and pigment-free controls. Note the observed colour in words, not only a swatch.
